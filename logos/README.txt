CLUB LOGOS
==========
Every team always has a crest: a generated one in the club's colours, so the app works offline.

To show real logos:

1. Automatic - once football-data.org is connected (Admin > Settings), the crest URL that
   comes with each fixture is downloaded once and cached on disk.

2. Manual - put PNG, SVG or WEBP files in this folder (or in the logos folder that the
   Settings page opens). Name each file after the team as the app spells it, e.g.

       Man City.png     Man United.svg     Nott'm Forest.png     Tottenham.webp

   Case, spaces and punctuation are ignored ("man-city.png" works too). Files here win over
   downloaded crests. Restart the app after adding files.

Club crests are trademarks of their clubs; use them in line with your licence/permissions.
